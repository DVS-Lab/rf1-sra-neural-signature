as reward paradigms. It includes three task-specific reward models, their 3×3
cross-decoding matrix, three primary leave-one-paradigm-out tests, and a pooled
common development reward signature. Every test participant is absent from all
training paradigms. UGR is a broader social valuation/context probe and never trained.

A separate secondary decision/context family includes clean Shared Reward decision,
Trust choice, and Doors decision maps; its own models/matrix/common signature,
bidirectional reward/context applications, and spatial similarities are implemented.

reports exact overlaps and stops before selection. Legacy single-task splits are
not silently reused. Current template checks include Trust choice COPEs 1–3 and
separate Doors decision COPE3. All prior holdout/source/privacy guards are retained.
