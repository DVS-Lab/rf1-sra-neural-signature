# RF1-SRA trans-task pilot — real analysis not yet run

The pipeline estimates common social-reward information across Shared Reward,
Trust, and Social/monetary Doors, with a separate secondary decision/context family
and UGR boundary probes. No real RF1 split, neural estimate, or performance is claimed.

The launcher requires at least 250 multitask-complete participants, locks exactly
50 before modeling, and replaces this placeholder with both cross-decoding matrices,
three reward leave-one-paradigm-out tests, common-signature probes, weight comparisons,
reliability, and aggregate cohort/provenance information. Holdout scoring is absent.

See `docs/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`, `docs/HOLDOUT_POLICY.md`, and `README.md`.
